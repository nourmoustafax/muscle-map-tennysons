## What BodyParts3D can contribute

The description supports an educational anatomical reference: a free three-dimensional whole-body model of an **adult human male**, usable as an electronic atlas to confirm the position and shape of organs. It also describes selecting body parts and changing viewpoint, zoom, color, and opacity to generate an anatomical image through Anatomogram. [1]

For computed work, the description specifically supports reusing the available data as input to **human-body simulation and data mapping**. That supports atlas visualization and geometry/reference mapping in a personal-monitoring prototype. It does not establish that BodyParts3D is an individual’s measured anatomy, a longitudinal measurement source, or a clinical result. [1]

## Limitations and caveats

- The source describes an adult human male whole-body model. It does not establish subject-specific anatomy, population coverage, pathology coverage, or comparability across people.
- The two BodyParts3D pages do **not** state the source equipment, acquisition modality or protocol, scan settings, segmentation method, spatial resolution, calibration, measurement error, or cross-device comparability. No equipment or acquisition claim should be inferred from this atlas description. [1] [2]
- The pages do not state clinical validation, diagnostic or therapeutic suitability, ethics/IRB approval, or a professional-training requirement. They therefore provide neither clinical approval nor a training credential; those topics remain outside what these sources verify.
- CC BY 4.0 is offered as-is and without warranties, including accuracy or fitness for a particular purpose. Other rights, including privacy, publicity, moral rights, patent, or trademark rights, may still limit a particular use, and the license does not grant implied endorsement. [3] [4]
- Redistribution is allowed under the current license, but attribution and change indication remain required when sharing licensed or adapted material. Do not assume that a third-party copy carries the current terms; use the dated official license page above. [2]
